Ammar Yassir (b.2005) is a Sudanese documentary photographer. He specializes in telling stories tied to the ongoing war in Sudan. His work focuses on the idea of belonging and the connection between humans and their spaces. His work is centered on his own lived experience with displacement; he focuses on telling stories about refugees and IDPS. He was named the youngest recipient of the Ian Parry Grant in 2024 and the youngest FOAM Talent in 2026. and DIE ZEIT and Internazionale, among others, published his work.
